Say an AI writes you a poem. Fine, poems evaporate.

Now imagine one that files your taxes, cancels your flight, and actually refunds the miles.

Microsoft shipped Agent Framework skills yesterday. Basically a manual for your coding agent that says "here's how to not wire your tools backwards." That's a gift. Also, draw your own conclusions about what was happening before.

U Mobile in Malaysia seems to have cracked something real. Their AiDaH agents run 150+ operational scenarios on Bedrock AgentCore and cut some ticket categories by 84%. The machine checks configs, verifies eligibility, finds the break, proposes a fix. All before a human opens their eyes.

Here's what nobody puts in the press release: content is forgiving. Actions leave receipts. Every decision an agent makes is a liability with a timestamp, sitting in a log someone will read at 2am while a manager asks why the warehouse shut down.

The hard skill now is building agents you can explain to a regulator, debug when everything's on fire, and gracefully shut down when they get creative about what "refund" means. Everything else is just wiring.
