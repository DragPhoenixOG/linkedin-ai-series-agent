Anthropic shipped Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 on November 9. Fable handles general work. Mythos stays gated behind verification programs for cybersecurity and life sciences research.

The pricing is concrete. Eight dollars per million input tokens, forty for output. Speed claims are concrete. Everything else in the announcement - formalizing Fermat's Last Theorem, solving open physics problems - is Anthropic's own testing. Your mileage will vary.

I keep noticing the data retention clause. Zero retention only if you qualify and request it, and only guaranteed through June 2027. Otherwise, thirty days with human safety review. Procurement reads the fine print before the compliance team asks why customer data is sitting in someone else's logs.

The cadence is the real story. Another model version, another regression cycle, another set of prompts to validate. The gap between benchmark brags and production reliability stays wide. Anthropic's tests run on curated tasks. Your agents run on real user input that misspells "refund," asks for refunds that don't match policy, and hopes you don't notice.

Prompt testing, structured outputs, and having a real plan for when the model goes off-script. Those are still your job. The new weights just change how fast the surprises arrive.
